# Bar and Clubhouse

The clubhouse has three areas with different characters and, deliberately, different standards.

## The spike bar

The spike bar is the room next to the locker rooms. It exists so that players coming off the course can have a drink without changing, and it is intentionally informal.

**Golf shoes are permitted in the spike bar.** So is wet weather gear. The floor is tiled for exactly this reason.

**Denim is acceptable in the spike bar.** This is a longstanding local arrangement and reflects the fact that the room is used by members who have not played that day, by contractors, and by the greenkeeping staff at the end of a shift. Members are asked to use their judgement about what is reasonable.

Food service in the spike bar is limited to the bar menu — sandwiches, toasties, the soup of the day.

## The main lounge

Smart casual. Collared shirts, no golf shoes, no hats. The lounge is where the club takes visiting captains and where members bring family, and the standard reflects that.

Denim is not appropriate in the lounge.

## The dining room

The dining room is used for club dinners, the prizegiving, and Sunday lunch. Jacket required for club dinners; smart casual otherwise. The dining room is bookable for private functions through the Bar Manager.

## Bar hours

| | |
|---|---|
| Monday to Thursday | 11:00 – 22:00 |
| Friday to Sunday | 11:00 – 23:00 |

Last orders fifteen minutes before close. The bar will stay open later for club functions and for the evening of the Club Championship, at the Bar Manager's discretion.

The bar does not open before 11:00 even when the course opens earlier. Members wanting coffee before a morning round should use the machine in the spike bar, which is available from 07:30.

## Accounts

Bar purchases may be charged to a member's account. Accounts are settled monthly. A member whose bar account exceeds $400 will be asked to settle it before further charges are permitted.

Guests may not run an account. A member signing in a guest is responsible for the guest's bar charges if they are put on account.

## Functions and private hire

The dining room and the lounge may be hired by members for private functions. Enquiries to the Bar Manager. The course and the spike bar are not available for private hire at any time — the spike bar in particular must remain available to members coming off the course.
