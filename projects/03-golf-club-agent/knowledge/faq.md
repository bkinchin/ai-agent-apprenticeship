# Frequently Asked Questions

**How far ahead can I book a tee time?**
Six weeks. The tee sheet opens at midnight, six weeks to the day.

**How many bookings can I hold at once?**
Two live bookings at any time. A booking stops counting once it has been played.

**Can I book for someone else?**
You can book a tee time that includes other members, but the booking is in your name and you are responsible for it. You cannot make a booking on another member's behalf in their name.

**What happens if I cancel?**
Free up to 24 hours before your tee time. Inside 24 hours a late cancellation fee applies. The fee exists because a slot released at short notice is very often wasted, particularly on a Saturday.

**Can I bring a guest?**
Yes, subject to your category's monthly allowance. Guests must be accompanied by you at all times, and the green fee is charged to your account.

**What is the guest green fee?**
Please check with the pro shop — the fee was reviewed recently and this page may not reflect the current figure.

**Can my guest play without me?**
No. A guest must be accompanied by their sponsoring member.

**Is there a dress code?**
Yes, on the course, on the practice ground and in the clubhouse. The main points are a collared shirt, tailored shorts or trousers, and no denim. The spike bar has its own more relaxed standard.

**What time does the club close?**
The clubhouse closes at 10:30pm.

**When is the course closed?**
The tee sheet is closed to general booking during Saturday competition mornings. The course itself may be closed at short notice for frost, waterlogging, or greens maintenance — the pro shop will post this on the website and the club's social media as early as possible.

**Can I hire a buggy?**
Yes, from the pro shop. Buggies are limited in number and are not bookable in advance except on medical grounds, in which case speak to the Pro Shop Manager.

**Can I hire clubs?**
Yes, a limited number of sets are available from the pro shop.

**Do you offer lessons?**
Yes. Individual and group lessons are available with the club professional. Book through the pro shop.

**I've lost something on the course.**
Lost property is held in the pro shop for three months.

**How do I join?**
You need a proposer and a seconder, both existing members. Speak to the Club Secretary, who will explain the process and the current position on availability.

**Can I pay my subscription in instalments?**
Speak to the Secretary. The committee would rather agree an arrangement than lose a member.

**How do I get a handicap?**
Submit three cards from qualifying rounds, marked by a member. The Handicap Secretary will then issue an initial handicap.

**Who do I speak to about the condition of the course?**
The pro shop, who will pass it to the greens team. Please do not raise it directly with staff working on the course.

**Can I play on my own?**
Yes, but a single player has no standing on the course and should be prepared to be joined or to let groups through. The pro shop will usually try to pair you up on a busy morning.

**Is the course walkable?**
Yes. The 11th and 12th involve a climb that some members find hard going in summer; buggies are available.

**Are there caddies?**
No. The club has not had a caddie programme for some years.

**Can I use a push trolley in winter?**
Yes, unless trolleys are suspended for ground conditions, which the pro shop will post at the first tee and on the website. Trolley bans are usually lifted within a day or two.

**What happens if there is frost?**
The course may open late, open to temporary greens, or stay closed. The decision is made by the greens staff at first light and posted as early as possible. Bookings affected by a closure are cancelled without charge.

**Do you have a driving range?**
There is a practice ground with grass tees, not a floodlit range. It closes when the pro shop closes.

**Is there a putting green?**
Yes, beside the first tee, and a short game area beside the 10th for chipping and bunker practice.

**Can I store my clubs at the club?**
A limited number of lockers are available. There is a waiting list. Speak to the Secretary.

**Do you have Wi-Fi?**
Yes, in the clubhouse. The password is on the noticeboard by the bar.

**Can I bring children into the clubhouse?**
Yes. Children under 16 must be accompanied by an adult and are not permitted at the bar itself. Family lunch on a Sunday is popular and booking is advised.
