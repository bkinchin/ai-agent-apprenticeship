# Competition Rules

> **Note:** this document is overdue for review. Members should check current arrangements with the Competition Secretary.

## Entry

Entry to club competitions is open to full, country and midweek members holding an active club handicap. Midweek members may enter midweek competitions only.

Entry is by the sheet in the pro shop or through the online system. Entries close at 17:00 on the Thursday before a Saturday competition.

A member who enters and does not play, without withdrawing before the entry deadline, may be recorded as a no-show. Three no-shows in a season results in a written warning from the committee.

## Handicaps and allowances

Competition handicaps are calculated under the **CONGU Standard Scratch and handicapping system**. Members' exact handicaps are rounded to the nearest whole number for competition purposes, and playing handicap allowances are applied as follows:

| Format | Allowance |
|---|---|
| Singles strokeplay | Full handicap |
| Singles matchplay | Full difference |
| Fourball betterball | 3/4 handicap |
| Foursomes | 1/2 combined |

Buffer zones apply as per the CONGU categories. A member returning a score within their buffer zone retains their handicap; outside it, the handicap is adjusted at 0.1 per stroke above.

## The Club Championship

The Club Championship is played over 36 holes on a single day in July, scratch, with a separate handicap prize. Entry is restricted to full and country members.

Distance measuring devices are not permitted.

The tee sheet for the Championship is closed to general booking for the day; the exact window is held in the booking rules.

## Saturday medals

The Saturday medal is played on competition Saturdays. **The competition window and which Saturdays it applies to are held in the club's booking rules, not here** — this document describes the format, not the schedule.

Cards must be signed by the marker and returned to the box in the pro shop within thirty minutes of finishing. A card returned late may be disqualified at the Competition Secretary's discretion.

## Ties

Ties in strokeplay competitions are decided on the last nine, last six, last three, last one. Where a tie remains, the Competition Secretary will determine the result by lot.

## Disputes

A player in doubt about a rule should play a second ball under Rule 20.1c and report the facts to the Competition Secretary before signing their card. A dispute raised after the card has been signed will not normally be entertained.

The committee's decision is final.
