# Membership Handbook

## Membership categories

The club offers five categories of membership. Current fees are held separately and are the authoritative figures — this handbook describes what each category *means*, not what it costs.

### Full membership

Unrestricted playing rights, seven days a week, subject to the competition calendar. Full members may enter all club competitions, hold a handicap through the club, and vote at the AGM. Full members have priority on the tee sheet during the summer twilight period.

Full membership carries the largest guest allowance and is the only category permitted to sponsor a new member.

### Country membership

Intended for members whose primary residence is more than 100km from the club. Country members have the same playing rights as full members but a reduced guest allowance, and are not eligible to vote at the AGM.

The 100km rule is applied on joining and is not re-tested annually. Members who subsequently move closer are expected to notify the Secretary, though in practice this is rarely done and the committee has historically taken a relaxed view.

### Midweek membership

Playing rights Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays. Midweek members may play at weekends as a guest of a full member, in which case the standard guest green fee applies and the booking counts against the sponsoring member's guest allowance rather than being treated as a member round.

Midweek members may enter midweek competitions but not the Club Championship.

### Junior membership

Available to players under a specified age. Junior members play free on weekdays outside peak periods and have limited weekend access.

### Social membership

Clubhouse and bar access only. Social members may not book tee times, enter competitions, or hold a handicap. Social members may be signed in as a guest by a playing member in the normal way, in which case the guest green fee applies.

---

## Joining

Applications are made to the Club Secretary. A prospective member must be sponsored by a full member and seconded by a second member of any playing category. The application is posted on the clubhouse noticeboard for fourteen days, during which any member may object in writing to the Secretary.

The committee considers applications at its monthly meeting. There is currently no waiting list for midweek, junior or social categories. Full and country membership have had a waiting list in the past; prospective members should ask the Secretary about current availability.

A joining fee is payable on acceptance and is not refundable. Annual subscriptions run from 1 September and are not pro-rated for members joining part-way through the year, except that members joining after 1 May pay half the annual subscription for the remainder of that year.

---

## Guests

Members may sign in guests subject to their category allowance. The guest green fee is currently **$15 per guest per round**, payable by the sponsoring member and charged to their account.

A guest may play the course no more than six times in any calendar year, regardless of which member signs them in. This is intended to prevent regular players avoiding membership; the committee will write to any guest approaching this limit and invite them to apply.

Guests must be accompanied on the course by their sponsoring member at all times. A member who signs in a guest is responsible for that guest's conduct, their observance of the dress code, and any damage to the course.

Guests may use the practice facilities and the clubhouse on the day they play. Guests may not use the practice facilities on days they are not playing.

---

## Subscriptions and arrears

Subscriptions fall due on 1 September. A member whose subscription remains unpaid on 1 October is suspended from playing and from the tee sheet until the account is settled. Suspended members retain clubhouse access.

The committee may, at its discretion, agree an instalment arrangement with a member in difficulty. Members are encouraged to speak to the Secretary early rather than allow arrears to accumulate; the club has no interest in losing members over timing.

---

## Resignation and transfer

A member wishing to resign must notify the Secretary in writing before 1 August, failing which the following year's subscription becomes payable in full. This is applied strictly — the committee has upheld it on appeal on several occasions.

Membership is personal and may not be transferred, sold, or lent. A member found to have allowed another person to play on their membership will be referred to the committee.

---

## Handicaps

Playing members other than social members are entitled to hold a club handicap. Handicaps are maintained by the Handicap Secretary in accordance with the national system.

Members must submit cards from qualifying rounds. A member who does not return a qualifying score within a rolling twelve-month period may have their handicap marked inactive, in which case they may still play socially but may not enter competitions until it is reinstated.
