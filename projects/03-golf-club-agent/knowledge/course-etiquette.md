# Course Etiquette and Local Rules

## Pace of play

The course should be played in three hours fifteen minutes for a fourball and two hours forty-five for a two-ball. Groups are expected to keep pace with the group ahead, not merely to stay ahead of the group behind — this distinction is the single most common cause of congestion.

A group that loses a clear hole should invite the following group through, whether or not they are searching for a ball. This is not a criticism; it is simply how the course keeps moving on a busy Saturday.

Ready golf is encouraged in social play. Play when you are ready and it is safe to do so, rather than waiting strictly on honour.

## Care of the course

- Replace divots on the fairway. On the tees, use the sand and seed provided.
- Repair your pitch mark on the green, and one other. The greens staff ask that members use a proper repair tool rather than a tee.
- Rake bunkers thoroughly. Leave the rake inside the bunker, laid flat, parallel to the line of play.
- Do not take trolleys between a bunker and the green.
- Buggies must keep to the paths on the approach to greens and tees, and off the course entirely when the ground is soft.

## The greenkeeping staff

Greenkeeping staff have right of way at all times. Wait until they signal you through and until they have moved to a safe position. Do not play towards a working machine under any circumstances.

Members with a concern about course condition should raise it through the pro shop rather than with the staff on the course. The greens team work to a programme agreed with the committee, and a member expressing a strong view directly to a staff member puts that person in an impossible position.

## Practice

The practice ground is open during pro shop hours. Members may not practise on the course itself at any time, including chipping to greens outside play. The short game area beside the 10th is available for chipping and bunker practice.

Balls used on the practice ground are club property and must not be taken onto the course.

## Local rules

**Out of bounds** is defined by the white posts on the 3rd, 7th and 14th, and by the boundary fence along the 12th.

**The ditch** crossing the 5th and 6th is a penalty area, marked yellow.

**Ground under repair** is marked in blue. Relief is free.

**The practice ground** is out of bounds for play from any hole.

**Stones in bunkers** may be removed as movable obstructions. This is a local rule adopted after a member was injured in 2019.

**Distance measuring devices** are permitted in all club competitions except the Club Championship, where the committee has retained the prohibition.

## Safety

Do not play until the group ahead is out of range. On the 4th and the 11th, where the landing area is blind, ring the bell before playing your approach.

If your ball may strike another player, shout "fore" immediately and loudly. Members are asked not to be embarrassed about this — a shout that turns out to be unnecessary costs nothing.

Lightning: the course siren is one long blast to suspend play and two short blasts to resume. On hearing the siren, leave the course immediately by the shortest safe route. Shelters are located behind the 6th tee and beside the 13th green. The club's position is that no round is worth a risk and members who continue to play after the siren may be asked to leave the course.
