## Answer structure

For any senior technical question:

1. **Clarify** — "What's the blast radius? Is the action reversible?"
2. **Take a position** — not a survey of options
3. **Name the tradeoff** — what your choice costs
4. **Give the evidence** — how you would know you were right
5. **Say what would change your mind**

Point 5 is what marks out a senior engineer. Certainty without conditions reads as inexperience.

## The 21 questions

One per curriculum day. Write out each answer properly — the act of writing is the preparation. Skimming and thinking "I could answer that" is not.

## Depth, not recall

Senior interviews follow a question with three escalating follow-ups. Recall gets you through the first. Only having built the thing gets you through the third. Every position you hold should trace to something you measured.
